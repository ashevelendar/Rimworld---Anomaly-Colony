# Rift Materials

## Design Philosophy

Rift materials should feel like substances produced by an anomalous ecosystem rather than recoloured vanilla resources.

The material set should remain relatively small. Each resource should have a clear purpose and should contribute to the identity of a Sightstealer colony.

## 1. Riftstone

**Role:** Primary Rift construction material.

**Source:** Mined from Riftstone deposits in the Rift biome; may also appear as chunks.

**Appearance:** Nearly black stone with subtle grey, violet, or blue veins. Surfaces should look slightly warped or melted rather than resembling black granite.

**Gameplay concept:** Extremely durable against ordinary physical damage, making it an excellent defensive building material. It should be notably vulnerable to fire compared with its physical durability.

**Psychological effect:** Large structures or enclosed rooms built from Riftstone can impose a low-level psychological disturbance on ordinary non-Sightstealer pawns nearby. Sightstealers are unaffected.

**Potential uses:**
- Walls.
- Doors.
- Floors.
- Furniture.
- Defensive structures.
- Rift architecture.

## 2. Umbral Shard

**Role:** Rare crystalline Rift resource.

**Source:** Rare deposits in the Rift biome; potentially produced in small amounts by selected advanced Rift plants or structures.

**Appearance:** Jagged black crystals with faint internal violet/blue light. The glow should be subtle and should not function as a normal light source.

**Potential uses:**
- Advanced Rift buildings.
- Teleportation structures.
- Psychic/ritual structures.
- High-tier crafting.
- Special storyteller rewards.

## 3. Shadow Resin

**Role:** Organic Rift construction and crafting material.

**Source:** Harvested from selected Rift plants, particularly Bleakvine and Hollowtree; potentially obtained from selected Rift organisms.

**Appearance:** Thick black-purple resin resembling tar, with a slightly wet or organic appearance.

**Potential uses:**
- Furniture.
- Ritual structures.
- Darkness-generating structures.
- Advanced crafting.
- Biological building components.

## 4. Twisted Fibre

**Role:** Rift equivalent of a renewable textile/fibre resource.

**Source:** Harvested from Gloomroot, Umbral Reed, and other fibrous Rift plants.

**Appearance:** Black-grey fibres resembling a mixture of roots, sinew, and fungal threads.

**Potential uses:**
- Beds.
- Textile items.
- Furniture.
- Bags and storage items.
- Clothing.
- Selected buildings.

## 5. Void Sap

**Role:** Valuable plant-derived resource.

**Source:** Harvested or extracted from mature Shadowcap and Hollowtree plants, with appropriate processing if needed.

**Appearance:** Pitch-black liquid with tiny violet highlights.

**Potential uses:**
- Medicine.
- Psychic consumables.
- Ritual ingredients.
- High-tier crafting.
- Fuel for selected Rift structures.

## 6. Dread Petals

**Role:** Psychic ritual reagent.

**Source:** Harvested from mature Dread Bloom plants.

**Appearance:** Dark flower petals with black/purple colouring and an unsettling organic structure.

**Potential uses:**
- Psychic rituals.
- Ritual quality modifiers.
- Favour-related rituals.
- Special psychic consumables.

Normal pawns interacting with large quantities may receive minor psychological effects if appropriate.

## 7. Condensed Void

**Role:** Extremely rare end-game Rift material.

**Source:** Very rare Rift deposits, advanced Rift structures, major Anomaly events, or exceptional rewards from The Dark One.

**Appearance:** A small black mass or sphere surrounded by subtle visual distortion, as though the space around it is bending.

**Potential uses:**
- End-game Rift structures.
- Advanced teleportation.
- Powerful psychic structures.
- Exceptional rituals.
- Rare storyteller rewards.

This resource should remain scarce and should not appear as an ordinary early-game mining resource.

## Material Relationships

The intended progression is:

**Riftstone** = common defensive construction.

**Twisted Fibre / Shadow Resin** = common renewable biological crafting resources.

**Umbral Shard / Void Sap** = valuable mid-to-late game resources.

**Dread Petals** = specialised psychic/ritual resource.

**Condensed Void** = rare end-game resource.

## Fire and Psychological Trade-offs

Rift materials should not simply be universally better than vanilla materials.

Suggested balance:

| Material | Physical durability | Fire resistance | Psychological effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riftstone | Extremely high | Poor | Unease |
| Shadow Resin | Moderate | Poor | Low |
| Twisted Fibre | Low/moderate | Very poor | Low |
| Umbral Shard | High | High | Dread |
| Void Sap | Not normally structural | N/A | Situational |
| Dread Petals | Not structural | Low | Psychic |
| Condensed Void | Extremely high for advanced structures | Very high | Severe |

The psychological effects should be implemented through appropriate RimWorld thought/hediff mechanics rather than creating an unnecessarily complex custom mental-health framework.
