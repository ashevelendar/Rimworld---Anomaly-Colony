# The Umbral Truth

## Overview

The Sightstealer colony's ideology should feel like a coherent worldview built around the Anomaly rather than a generic dark cult. Its central belief is that ordinary reality is incomplete and that the anomalous state beneath it is the deeper truth.

The ideology should reinforce the colony's existing mechanics: darkness, corpses, sacrifice, psychic rituals, the Rift, and Sightstealer superiority.

The exact implementation should use RimWorld Ideology systems where practical, with custom code only where the required behaviour cannot be expressed through standard memes, precepts, rituals, roles, or thoughts.

---

## Ideology Name

**The Umbral Truth**

Possible description:

> Reality is a thin veil. Beneath it lies the anomaly, the darkness, and the truth. The dead return to that truth. The living may offer themselves to it. Those who embrace the darkness are closer to what the universe truly is.

---

# Main Meme

## Anomalous

**Primary ideological theme.**

The anomaly is not regarded as an aberration to be feared or cured. It is regarded as the underlying truth of existence.

Core beliefs:

- Anomalous phenomena are sacred or worthy of reverence.
- Psychic phenomena are desirable.
- Anomaly rituals are important communal acts.
- Strange or impossible phenomena should provoke fascination rather than disgust.
- The Monolith and other major anomalous objects can be treated as sacred or significant, subject to the eventual Anomaly integration design.

This is the defining meme of the ideology and should remain the central theme even if optional supporting memes are removed.

---

# Supporting Memes

## 1. Darkness Worship

Darkness is considered the natural and proper state of existence. Light represents the intrusive surface world rather than the deeper truth.

### Suggested Precepts

| Subject | Suggested Belief |
|---|---|
| Darkness | Loved / highly preferred |
| Being in darkness | Strong positive mood |
| Bright light | Disliked |
| Extreme brightness | Hated |
| Artificial light | Disliked |
| Working in darkness | Preferred |
| Darkness-based rituals | Highly valued |
| Building in darkness | Preferred |
| Sightstealer invisibility | Revered / desirable |

### Gameplay intention

This meme should reinforce the existing Sightstealer light mechanics. Sightstealer colonists should naturally prefer the environments in which they are mechanically strongest.

---

## 2. Corpse Reverence

The ideology should not describe corpse consumption simply as cannibalism. The preferred interpretation is that the dead have returned to the Rift and their remains are being returned to the darkness.

### Suggested Precepts

| Subject | Suggested Belief |
|---|---|
| Eating human corpses | Acceptable / preferred |
| Eating animal corpses | Preferred |
| Eating Twisted Meat | Loved / highly preferred |
| Seeing corpses | Positive |
| Leaving usable corpses unconsumed | Mildly negative |
| Butchering corpses | No issue |
| Corpse disposal | Disliked |
| Normal human food | Unimportant or undesirable for Sightstealers |

### Gameplay intention

This should reinforce the colony's corpse-only/Twisted Meat diet. Corpses are not a disgusting waste product to Sightstealers. They are a resource and, ideologically, a return to the darkness.

The existing custom food restriction remains the mechanical authority. The ideology should complement it rather than replace it.

---

## 3. Sacrifice

Life has value partly because life can be offered.

Sacrifice is one of the most important religious acts available to the colony and should connect directly to the existing psychic sacrifice/reproduction system and The Dark One's Favour system.

### Suggested Precepts

| Subject | Suggested Belief |
|---|---|
| Human sacrifice | Required / highly respected |
| Prisoner sacrifice | Highly positive |
| Witnessing sacrifice | Positive |
| Performing a sacrifice | Strong positive |
| Refusing a required sacrifice | Negative |
| Sacrificing a worthy individual | Very positive |
| Sacrificial ritual quality | Important |
| Execution as sacrifice | Acceptable / preferred |

### Gameplay intention

A successful sacrifice should provide ideological approval while also being capable of increasing The Dark One's Favour according to the storyteller design.

Sacrifice should feel meaningful, not like a button pressed solely to create a new pawn.

---

## 4. Psychic Communion

The mind is considered another doorway into the anomaly.

### Suggested Precepts

| Subject | Suggested Belief |
|---|---|
| Psychic sensitivity | Positive |
| Psychic powers | Revered |
| Psycasters | Respected / revered |
| Psychic rituals | Highly valued |
| Successful psychic rituals | Strong positive |
| Poor or failed rituals | Negative |
| Developing psychic abilities | Strongly encouraged |

### Gameplay intention

This gives the colony a cultural reason to pursue psychic power rather than treating it merely as another RimWorld stat.

Where practical, ritual quality and psychic activity can also feed into The Dark One's Favour system.

---

## 5. The Rift

The Rift is considered the true home of the Sightstealer.

It is not merely a biome. It is the environment in which the colony is closest to its natural state.

### Suggested Precepts

| Subject | Suggested Belief |
|---|---|
| Living in the Rift | Very positive |
| Rift terrain | Beautiful |
| Rift plants | Beautiful |
| Rift architecture | Beautiful |
| Leaving the Rift | Negative |
| Returning to the Rift | Positive |
| Ordinary architecture | Unimpressive / ugly |
| Rift materials | Valued |
| Darkness within the Rift | Highly valued |

### Gameplay intention

A Sightstealer colony should feel increasingly attached to the Rift rather than simply using it as a convenient biome.

If the player eventually establishes a colony elsewhere, the ideology can create a meaningful cultural penalty for abandoning the Rift.

---

# Optional Extreme Meme

## 6. Sightstealer Supremacy

This meme is optional because it produces a substantially more hostile ideology toward outsiders.

The core belief is not merely that Sightstealers are different. It is that Sightstealers represent a more advanced or truthful form of existence.

> We are not aberrations. We are what remains when the veil is stripped away.

### Suggested Precepts

| Subject | Suggested Belief |
|---|---|
| Sightstealers | Revered |
| Humans | Inferior |
| Other xenotypes | Inferior / tolerated |
| Sightstealer leadership | Preferred / expected |
| Human leadership | Disliked |
| Recruiting humans | Acceptable |
| Converting humans | Preferred |
| Human prisoners | Acceptable |
| Sightstealer prisoners | Strongly undesirable |
| Human sacrifice | Acceptable / encouraged |

### Gameplay intention

This turns the colony into a genuinely Sightstealer society rather than a mixed human colony following an unusual religion.

It should remain optional so the player can create a less supremacist or more experimental Anomaly ideology if desired.

---

# Ideological Synergy

The memes should reinforce one another rather than functioning as isolated bonuses.

### Darkness + The Rift

The Rift provides the ideal environment for the ideology's most important environmental belief. Sightstealers are happier and mechanically stronger in the darkness that the ideology considers sacred.

### Corpse Reverence + Sacrifice

A sacrificed pawn dies as an intentional offering. The corpse then becomes part of the Sightstealer colony's food economy if desired.

This creates a deliberately alien cycle:

**Living outsider -> Sacrifice -> Death -> Return to the darkness -> Sustenance**

### Sacrifice + The Dark One

Sacrifice should satisfy both the ideology and The Dark One's favour system.

A high-quality sacrifice can therefore produce:

- ideological approval
- storyteller Favour
- potential reproduction
- potential storyteller reward

### Psychic Communion + Anomalous

Psychic activity is both religiously valuable and thematically consistent with the colony's relationship with the anomaly.

### Sightstealer Supremacy + The Rift

If the optional supremacy meme is selected, the Rift becomes not merely sacred territory but the natural homeland of the superior species.

---

# Relationship With The Dark One

The Dark One should function as the colony's storyteller and ideological patron without necessarily being implemented as a literal deity.

The ideology's interpretation is that The Dark One understands the anomalous truth and rewards those who embrace it.

This distinction is useful because it allows the storyteller to remain a gameplay system while still feeling integrated into the ideology.

The colony does not need to worship a literal god called The Dark One. Instead, colonists can regard The Dark One as a being, force, or intelligence aligned with the truth they already believe in.

This should make storyteller rewards feel like **recognition** rather than arbitrary loot.

# Recommended Starting Configuration

For the initial playable Sightstealer colony, use:

**Ideology:** The Umbral Truth

**Main meme:**
- Anomalous

**Supporting memes:**
- Darkness Worship
- Corpse Reverence
- Sacrifice
- Psychic Communion
- The Rift

**Optional:**
- Sightstealer Supremacy

This produces a coherent identity without requiring every possible mechanic to be mandatory.

---

# Implementation Notes

Do not attempt to implement every suggested precept as custom code immediately.

Prefer vanilla Ideology systems for:

- memes
- standard mood effects
- preferred/disliked activities
- corpse attitudes
- darkness-related thoughts where available
- rituals
- role expectations
- social preferences

Use custom C# only where the ideology needs to directly interact with mod-specific systems, such as:

- Sightstealer-specific darkness mechanics
- The Dark One's Visibility/Favour system
- Rift-specific buildings or resources
- custom sacrifice outcomes
- Sightstealer-only reactions

The ideology should remain functional even if optional advanced interactions are temporarily unavailable.

---

# Design Principle

The ideology should communicate one simple idea:

> **Reality is the lie. The darkness is the truth.**

Everything else follows from that belief.
