# Rift Architecture

## Design Philosophy

Sightstealer buildings should look like structures that belong to an anomalous organism rather than ordinary human buildings painted black.

Early structures can use Riftstone and biological materials in recognisable architectural forms. Advanced structures should gradually become more organic, unnatural, and integrated with the Rift ecosystem.

## Construction Materials

### Riftstone Construction

Riftstone should be a major construction material for:

- Walls.
- Doors.
- Floors.
- Basic furniture.
- Defensive structures.

Riftstone structures should have exceptionally high physical durability but poor resistance to fire.

Large amounts of Riftstone in a room or enclosed area can produce psychological discomfort in ordinary pawns. Sightstealers are unaffected.

## Proposed Buildings

### Rift Larder

Specialised storage for corpses and Twisted Meat.

The building should make corpse-based food storage feel intentional rather than like the colony is simply using a normal freezer in an unusual way.

### Rift Nest

Sightstealer sleeping/resting structure.

Potentially provides additional regeneration or darkness-related benefits while the pawn rests inside or nearby.

### Rift Altar

Dedicated structure for the Sightstealer sacrificial reproduction ritual.

A visually significant organic stone structure rather than a generic ritual spot.

### Umbral Hearth

Creates or maintains an area of darkness around itself.

Should be useful for establishing safe dark zones inside otherwise difficult environments.

### Shadow Veil

A defensive darkness structure.

Creates a local area of reduced light, potentially consuming a resource or requiring maintenance.

### Rift Maw

Corpse-processing structure.

Potentially converts corpses into useful Rift resources or processes them into a more efficient food/resource form.

This should remain balanced so it does not make ordinary corpse disposal obsolete for every colony.

### Void Sap Extractor

Processes suitable mature plants, especially Hollowtrees or Shadowcaps, to produce Void Sap.

### Obscuring Obelisk

Advanced structure that creates a large darkness/visibility suppression area.

Potentially useful for colony defence and for maintaining a dark perimeter.

### Shadow Gate

Teleportation-related structure.

A possible late-mid-game system for creating linked teleport destinations for Sightstealers.

### Rift Teleporter

More advanced teleportation infrastructure using Umbral Shards or Condensed Void.

Should be significantly more powerful than ordinary pawn movement but constrained by range, cooldown, destination requirements, or resource cost.

### Psychic Resonator

Advanced structure that interacts with psychic activity, rituals, Favour, or the storyteller.

Could provide temporary benefits or amplify certain Sightstealer abilities.

### Umbral Beacon

High-tier structure associated with The Dark One.

Could increase or manipulate storyteller interactions, but should have a meaningful downside such as increased Visibility or stronger Anomaly activity.

### Condensed Void Reactor

Late-game structure requiring Condensed Void.

Potentially powers advanced Rift infrastructure or provides a major colony benefit at significant risk.

## Psychological Architecture

Rift architecture should create a meaningful choice for colonies containing non-Sightstealer pawns.

### Suggested effects

**Riftstone:** low-level Unease.

**Umbral Shards:** stronger local Dread.

**Shadow Resin structures:** mild unsettling effects.

**Condensed Void structures:** severe psychological effects for ordinary pawns.

Sightstealers should be immune to these effects.

The exact implementation should prefer existing RimWorld thought/hediff systems rather than creating a large custom mood framework.

## Architectural Progression

### Early

The colony looks like a dark, hostile settlement:

- Riftstone walls.
- Basic doors.
- Riftstone floors.
- Gloomroot/Umbral Reed cultivation.
- Simple nests and corpse storage.

### Mid Game

The colony begins to look distinctly non-human:

- Organic structures.
- Bleakvine spreading around buildings.
- Rift Altars.
- Umbral Hearths.
- Shadow Veils.
- Rift Maws.
- More extensive Riftstone construction.

### Late Game

The colony begins to resemble a piece of the Rift itself:

- Large organic structures.
- Shadow Gates.
- Obscuring Obelisks.
- Psychic Resonators.
- Umbral Beacons.
- Condensed Void infrastructure.

The intended visual progression is:

**Building with the Rift -> living with the Rift -> becoming part of the Rift.**

## Defensive Design Philosophy

Rift architecture should be powerful but not universally superior to vanilla construction.

The primary balancing weaknesses are:

- Fire.
- Psychological effects on ordinary pawns.
- Resource scarcity for advanced structures.
- Potential Visibility increases from powerful anomalous infrastructure.

This should encourage mixed-material bases and make the colony's architecture a strategic decision rather than a straight upgrade.
