# Umbral Events

## Overview

The Sightstealer colony should not experience the same event ecosystem as a normal RimWorld colony. Its unusual biology, relationship with the Rift, and connection to The Dark One should produce events that make the colony feel fundamentally different during ordinary play.

These events are collectively called **Umbral Events**.

The central design principle is:

> The darkness notices things.

Umbral Events should create a mixture of opportunity, horror, ecological change, and escalating conflict. They should interact with Visibility, Favour, Sightstealer biology, the Rift, and The Purifiers rather than behaving as isolated random events.

The events should be implemented incrementally. V1 should prioritise events that can be built reliably from normal RimWorld event, pawn, map, incident, hediff, and faction systems.

## Event List

### 1. Shadow Visitors

Replace or supplement conventional wanderer-style recruitment opportunities with visitors appropriate to a Sightstealer colony.

Possible variants:

- A lone Sightstealer appears and asks to join.
- A wounded Sightstealer is discovered hiding near the colony.
- A Sightstealer child appears alone.
- A Sightstealer from elsewhere seeks refuge.
- A mysterious figure leaves an offering and disappears.
- A Sightstealer briefly observes the colony before vanishing.

Some variants should initially be ambiguous rather than immediately revealing their identity.

The event should feel like someone or something deliberately approaching the colony, rather than an ordinary traveller happening to wander past.

### 2. The Darkness Takes

A colonist temporarily disappears without a conventional death or kidnapping incident.

The pawn may:

- Reappear elsewhere on the map.
- Reappear underground or in a dark location.
- Return wounded.
- Return with a temporary or permanent effect.
- Return unable to explain what happened.
- Fail to return, depending on event severity.

The event should avoid guaranteeing a supernatural explanation every time. Uncertainty is part of the intended horror.

### 3. Umbral Bloom

Rift flora spontaneously appears inside or around the colony without being deliberately planted.

Possible behaviour:

- Gloomroot appears in dark ground.
- Bleakvine spreads onto nearby structures.
- Shadowcap appears indoors or in dark areas.
- Dread Bloom appears near corpses or anomalous locations.
- Hollowtree begins growing in an unusual location.

The player can remove the growth or allow it to remain.

Allowing it may provide resources, ecological benefits, or Favour. Removing it may be safer but should potentially forfeit those benefits.

### 4. Nightfall

A temporary anomalous darkness event in which the map becomes significantly darker than normal.

This is not a normal eclipse or weather event. The light itself begins to fail.

During Nightfall:

- Sightstealers receive major darkness-related advantages.
- Regeneration is improved.
- Invisibility becomes easier to maintain or trigger.
- Teleportation may receive improved range or other tactical benefits.
- Rift flora may grow rapidly.
- The Dark One may gain Favour.
- Anomaly entities may emerge.

Normal colonies should experience Nightfall as dangerous or unsettling. Sightstealer colonies should recognise it as an opportunity.

The end of Nightfall may leave behind changed terrain, Rift flora, resources, or another minor consequence.

### 5. A Gift From The Dark

The Dark One occasionally leaves something for the colony, especially when Favour is high.

Possible gifts include:

- Rift resources.
- Umbral Shards.
- Void Sap or related materials.
- Healing or regeneration effects.
- Useful equipment.
- Rare Anomaly resources.
- Useful animals.
- Darkness-related temporary benefits.
- Psychic benefits.
- Special opportunities.

The presentation should feel like communication from an interested force rather than a conventional quest reward.

Not every gift needs to be conventionally useful. Rarely, The Dark One may leave something strange or ambiguous simply to establish its personality.

### 6. Purifier Hunts

At sufficient Visibility, The Purifiers should receive event variants designed specifically to counter Sightstealers rather than simply generating ordinary raids.

Examples:

#### Lightbearer Expedition

A Purifier force arrives equipped with portable light sources and attempts to illuminate the colony and its approaches.

#### Burning Expedition

Purifiers deliberately target Rift vegetation, Shadow Resin structures, and other flammable colony infrastructure in an attempt to burn the Rift influence away.

#### The Capture

A Purifier force attempts to capture one or more Sightstealers alive rather than simply killing them.

These events should become more likely and more sophisticated as Visibility rises.

### 7. The Rift Answers

At sufficiently high Favour, the Rift or The Dark One may respond directly to the colony's behaviour.

Possible effects include:

- Healing injured Sightstealers.
- Temporary regeneration improvements.
- Rapid Rift plant growth.
- Resource gifts.
- Temporary psychic benefits.
- A temporary colony-wide darkness effect.
- A passive or friendly Anomaly creature appearing.
- A new Rift feature or structure appearing.

This is the positive counterpart to The Darkness Takes and other unsettling Umbral Events.

### 8. Corpse Events

The Sightstealer relationship with corpses should create events that reinforce their unusual diet and culture.

Possible events include:

#### The Feast

A large number of corpses appear at the edge of the map with no clear explanation. The corpses may be human, animal, or mixed depending on event severity.

The event should create an immediate resource opportunity for a colony whose food economy depends on corpses.

Other corpse-focused variants may involve bodies appearing in unusual locations, unexpected corpse caches, or The Dark One drawing attention to available remains.

The previously proposed **Corpse Offering** variant is intentionally excluded. The colony should not receive a guaranteed high-value human corpse as a direct ritual-room offering.

### 9. Darkness Migration

Wildlife may become attracted to the colony's unusual darkness and Rift influence.

Possible outcomes include unusual groups of animals arriving, particularly nocturnal, anomalous, or darkness-adapted creatures.

Depending on the event and storyteller state, animals may be:

- Passive.
- Manhunter.
- Tameable or unusually easy to tame.
- Drawn toward Rift vegetation.
- Associated with an Anomaly event.

The event should help make the colony's local ecology diverge from that of ordinary settlements.

### 10. The Long Day

**The Long Day** is the direct opposite of Nightfall.

For a period of time, the sun refuses to set.

The map experiences continuous daylight for the duration of the event. This is not merely a normal long summer day. It is an anomalous period in which night does not arrive as expected.

The Long Day is intended to be one of the most dangerous environmental events for a Sightstealer colony.

During The Long Day:

- Darkness-based Sightstealer advantages are severely reduced or unavailable.
- Invisibility becomes much harder or impossible in exposed areas.
- Regeneration is reduced or disabled outside sufficiently dark locations.
- Light-sensitive penalties remain active continuously.
- Rift flora may suffer, stop growing, or become vulnerable.
- Dark-dependent buildings and systems may become less effective.
- The colony's normal combat tactics become significantly harder to use.

The player should be encouraged to respond by creating artificial darkness, moving underground, using enclosed structures, and making tactical use of shadows.

The Long Day should be frightening precisely because it removes the environmental advantage that Sightstealers normally take for granted.

It should be possible to survive, but it should feel like the world has temporarily become hostile to the colony's biology.

## Design Principles

### Events Should Interact With Core Systems

Umbral Events should make use of existing systems wherever possible:

- Visibility influences hostile events.
- Favour influences beneficial events.
- Darkness affects the strength and consequences of events.
- Rift flora and materials create event targets and rewards.
- Sightstealer biology changes how events are experienced.
- The Purifiers exploit known weaknesses.

### Avoid Generic Event Reskins

A normal wanderer with a different name is not an Umbral Event.

A normal raid with slightly darker clothing is not a Purifier Hunt.

Each event should either introduce a new decision, exploit a Sightstealer weakness, reinforce the colony's unusual economy/ecology, or advance its relationship with the Rift and The Dark One.

### Horror Through Uncertainty

Not every event should explain itself.

Some Umbral Events should deliberately leave the player asking what actually happened.

### Balance

Umbral Events should not simply make the colony permanently stronger. Positive events should create opportunities, while negative events should create genuine threats.

Nightfall should be a powerful advantage with consequences.

The Long Day should be a serious weakness without being an automatic death sentence.

## Implementation Priority

Recommended initial implementation order:

1. Shadow Visitors.
2. Umbral Bloom.
3. Nightfall.
4. The Long Day.
5. A Gift From The Dark.
6. Corpse Events.
7. Darkness Migration.
8. Purifier Hunts.
9. The Rift Answers.
10. The Darkness Takes.

More elaborate variants can be added after the core event framework is stable.
