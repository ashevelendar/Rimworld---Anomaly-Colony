# The Dark One

## Overview

**The Dark One** is the custom storyteller for the Sightstealer Colony. It is intended to feel less like a conventional RimWorld storyteller and more like an intelligent, interested force watching the colony develop.

The storyteller should visually resemble a Sightstealer, using the vanilla Anomaly Sightstealer visual identity rather than introducing a separate creature design.

The Dark One's central philosophy is simple:

> The more the colony acts like a Sightstealer colony, the more the world notices. The more the world notices, the more dangerous life becomes. In return, The Dark One rewards the colony for embracing what it is.

## Storyteller Goals

- Make Anomaly threats relevant from the beginning of a Sightstealer colony.
- Do not require the player to activate or progress the Monolith before Anomaly danger can become significant.
- Reward behaviour that fits the Sightstealer identity, particularly killing and psychic sacrifice rituals.
- Create an escalating relationship between the colony and the outside world.
- Give the player meaningful choices between remaining hidden and deliberately becoming more notorious.
- Avoid simply making The Dark One a harder version of Randy Random.

## Core Values

The storyteller uses two conceptual values:

### Visibility

Visibility represents how much the outside world knows about, suspects, or has noticed the colony.

Visibility is primarily a danger/attention value. Higher Visibility causes stronger reactions from the world.

### Favour

Favour represents how pleased or interested The Dark One is with the colony.

Favour is primarily a reward value. It should influence beneficial events and rewards without initially becoming a manually spendable currency.

## Storyteller Personality

The Dark One should feel deliberate rather than completely random.

It should:

- Introduce Anomaly threats earlier than the standard Anomaly progression would normally suggest.
- Escalate danger in response to colony behaviour.
- Give the colony periods of relative quiet when it has remained hidden.
- Reward successful sacrifices and psychic rituals.
- Occasionally provide unusual gifts or opportunities when Favour is high.
- Use the colony's Visibility to determine how aggressively outsiders respond.

## Anomaly Threats Before the Monolith

The Dark One is specifically intended to make Anomaly threats part of the colony's story before normal Monolith progression would make them common.

The exact incident list and weighting should be tested rather than hard-coded immediately. Early threats should be survivable and should scale with the colony's development and Visibility.

Avoid making the early game an endless chain of major Anomaly threats. The goal is to establish that the colony is unusual, not to remove all breathing room.

## Visibility Feedback Loop

The intended storyteller loop is:

`Kill -> Visibility rises -> The world notices -> Threats become stronger -> The colony survives/fights -> more opportunities to kill -> Visibility rises again`

Alongside that:

`Kills -> Favour rises -> The Dark One rewards the colony`

and:

`Psychic rituals / sacrifices -> Favour rises strongly -> better rewards`

The colony can deliberately stop drawing attention and allow Visibility to decay.

## Reward Philosophy

Rewards should reinforce the colony's identity rather than simply handing out generic silver.

Possible rewards include:

- Rift resources.
- Useful animals.
- Healing or regeneration effects.
- Darkness-related temporary benefits.
- Psychic benefits.
- Rare Anomaly resources.
- Useful equipment.
- Rare Rift materials.
- Special opportunities or events at very high Favour.

Rewards should remain valuable without becoming an automatic replacement for normal colony progression.

## Design Principle

The Dark One should tempt the player into a dangerous relationship:

> Become more visible and gain greater attention, but also gain greater favour and stronger rewards.

The storyteller should therefore encourage the player to ask whether the next kill, sacrifice, or ritual is worth the consequences.
