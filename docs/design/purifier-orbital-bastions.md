# Purifier Orbital Bastions

## Overview

The Purifiers should have a limited orbital presence through Odyssey, while leaving the vanilla gravship system unchanged for now.

A small number of special Purifier orbital installations can exist around the world. These installations are intended to be extremely dangerous optional targets that offer exceptional rewards, particularly rewards that advance the Umbral/Rift progression.

The design goal is not to overhaul gravships. The player's gravships remain vanilla/Odyssey gravships. The mod instead adds Purifier-controlled orbital strongholds as special locations that become part of the Sightstealer colony's wider conflict with The Purifiers.

## Number and Availability

- Normally generate **2 Purifier Orbital Bastions**.
- Allow a maximum of **3 Bastions** on an appropriate world.
- Do not generate large numbers of orbital installations.
- Bastions are persistent locations rather than ordinary temporary events.
- They should not be trivial early-game destinations.
- Their discoverability and activity can be influenced by the colony's Visibility.

Some Purifier settlements can also exist on the ground. The orbital Bastions are an additional, limited layer of the faction's infrastructure.

## Purifier Orbital Bastion

The Bastion is a heavily fortified spacer-level Purifier military installation designed specifically to combat Anomaly threats and Sightstealers.

The visual and mechanical identity should be that of an advanced military stronghold rather than a primitive cult base.

The Bastion should feel like a Sightstealer dungeon: the player is voluntarily entering an environment deliberately engineered to negate the colony's strengths.

## Defensive Doctrine

The Purifiers know what Sightstealers are and have built their orbital infrastructure around fighting them.

### Illumination

The Bastion should be aggressively illuminated.

Possible defensive systems include:

- High-powered floodlights.
- Industrial lighting.
- Emergency lighting.
- Portable or mounted anti-invisibility illumination.
- UV or other specialised light equipment where technically feasible.

The objective is to deny Sightstealers the darkness they normally rely upon.

### Fire

The Bastion uses fire as an area-denial weapon.

Possible defences include:

- Flamethrowers.
- Incendiary launchers.
- Incendiary traps.
- Fire-based defensive zones.
- Fire-resistant Purifier equipment.
- Fire suppression systems used to prevent the player from exploiting fire against the station.

The Purifiers should deliberately burn or deny vulnerable areas rather than simply shooting at the player.

### Anti-Sightstealer Equipment

Elite Bastion defenders should have equipment specifically suited to countering Sightstealers.

Potential equipment includes:

- **Solar Lance** - powerful anti-Sightstealer weapon, with reduced relevance against ordinary targets.
- **Radiant Projector** - portable high-intensity light weapon that helps defeat darkness and invisibility advantages.
- **Purifier Hellgun** - elite spacer-level firearm.
- **Lightbearer Armour** - specialised golden Purifier armour with protection suited to fire and integrated illumination.

Where possible, existing vanilla/Odyssey equipment and systems should be reused rather than creating unnecessary duplicate mechanics.

## Bastion Alert State

Attacking a Bastion should not be a simple static raid map.

Once the installation detects an intruder, it can enter a **Bastion Alert** state.

Potential responses include:

- Sealing doors.
- Increasing illumination.
- Activating defensive turrets.
- Deploying additional Purifier soldiers.
- Activating fire-based area denial.
- Protecting or relocating valuable equipment.
- Increasing defensive pressure as the player penetrates deeper into the station.

This creates a risk/reward decision: escape with accessible loot or push deeper into the Bastion for the most valuable rewards.

## Bastion Structure

The installation should have progressively more dangerous areas.

### Outer Defence

- Standard Purifier soldiers.
- Strong lighting.
- Basic turrets and security.
- Initial resource and equipment caches.

### Inner Defence

- Elite Purifiers.
- Stronger anti-Sightstealer equipment.
- More aggressive fire and illumination.
- More valuable technology and resources.

### Extermination Vault

The deepest and most dangerous section should contain the strongest defenders and the most valuable rewards.

Potential defenders include:

- Elite Purifiers.
- Lightbearers.
- Flame Troopers.
- Exterminators.
- Heavy weapon specialists.
- High-quality defensive turrets.

The vault should function as the effective boss area of the Bastion.

## Purifier Commander

Each Bastion should have a special commander or commander-like elite pawn.

The commander should have:

- Excellent combat capability.
- Strong Shooting and/or Melee skills.
- High-quality spacer-level armour.
- Specialised anti-Sightstealer equipment.
- Strong traits and/or relevant combat abilities.

Defeating the commander should provide a meaningful reward, trophy, or unique piece of equipment rather than merely another ordinary corpse.

## Rewards

The Bastions are intended to be one of the strongest optional sources of high-tier resources and technology in the mod.

### Dark Archeotech

Bastions should provide a reliable source of **Dark Archeotech Shards**, potentially including:

- Multiple Dark Archeotech Shards per Bastion.
- A chance of more valuable Dark Archeotech components.
- High-tier Anomaly technology.

Dark Archeotech directly supports the Umbral endgame and Rift Gate progression.

### Purifier Technology

Potential unique or high-tier rewards include:

- Solar Lances.
- Radiant Projectors.
- Lightbearer Armour.
- Purifier Hellguns.
- Advanced incendiary equipment.
- High-quality spacer weapons.
- Portable or specialised illumination technology.

Some Purifier technology may be usable by the player after recovery.

### Extermination Core

Each Bastion may contain a unique **Extermination Core**.

The Extermination Core is a major piece of Purifier technology and can serve as a component or prerequisite for advanced Umbral technology.

Its exact implementation should be decided alongside the Umbral research and endgame systems.

### Purifier Archives

Bastions should contain valuable information about the Purifiers' understanding of the Sightstealers and the Rift.

Recovering the archives can provide:

- Umbral research progress.
- Information about Sightstealer physiology.
- Information about Rift locations.
- Information about Anomaly entities.
- Purifier doctrine and history.
- Potential information concerning The Dark One.

This makes the Bastions a source of knowledge as well as physical loot.

### Other Rewards

Additional rewards can include:

- Rare Rift materials.
- Advanced Anomaly resources.
- Large resource caches.
- Commander equipment.
- High-quality weapons and armour.
- Special trophies or unique Purifier items.

The overall reward should be sufficiently strong that attacking a Bastion is tempting despite its danger.

## Visibility Integration

Purifier Bastions should interact with **The Dark One's Visibility system**.

### Low Visibility

- Bastions exist but may be difficult to locate.
- Purifiers are not actively focused on the colony.
- Orbital reconnaissance can be limited.

### Medium Visibility

- The colony can discover the existence of Bastions.
- Rumours or reconnaissance may reveal orbital activity.
- Bastions can become more active against the colony.

### High Visibility

- Purifiers become increasingly aware of the colony.
- Bastions can conduct reconnaissance.
- Purifier attacks become more frequent or sophisticated.
- Anti-Sightstealer equipment becomes more prevalent.

### Maximum Visibility / Rift Gate

Once the Rift Gate is constructed, Visibility is forced to maximum and the Purifiers enter their highest level of awareness.

The Bastions should be considered fully alerted to the threat posed by the colony.

## Destroying the Bastions

The Bastions are optional strategic targets. Destroying them should provide a tangible long-term benefit without completely removing the Purifiers from the game.

If the player destroys **all existing Purifier Orbital Bastions**:

- Ground-based Purifier operations continue.
- The Purifiers are not permanently eliminated.
- Purifier orbital infrastructure is removed.
- The final Rift Gate defence becomes somewhat less dangerous.

### Rift Gate Defence Reduction

Destroying every Bastion before constructing the Rift Gate should reduce the **threat points assigned to Purifier raids during the 15-day Rift Gate defence**.

This should be a meaningful reduction, but not enough to trivialise the endgame.

The intended result is:

> **You can weaken the Purifiers before you make the world your enemy.**

The player therefore has a strategic choice:

- Ignore the Bastions and accept the full Purifier threat during the final defence.
- Risk attacking the Bastions early for powerful rewards and a less severe Purifier presence during the Rift Gate defence.

Destroying some, but not all, Bastions should provide their normal loot and reduce available Purifier infrastructure, but the special final-defence threat reduction should only activate when **all Bastions have been destroyed**.

## Design Principles

1. **Do not modify gravships themselves in the initial implementation.** Vanilla/Odyssey gravships remain intact.
2. Orbital Purifier infrastructure should be limited to a handful of locations.
3. Bastions should be extremely dangerous to attack.
4. The Purifiers should fight according to their established doctrine: light, fire, discipline, and advanced technology.
5. Rewards should be exceptional enough to justify the danger.
6. Dark Archeotech should connect the Bastions directly to the Umbral endgame.
7. Destroying all Bastions should have a meaningful strategic effect on the Rift Gate defence.
8. The Bastions should feel like the Purifiers' most secure and dangerous installations, not generic faction settlements.
9. Odyssey integration should expand the Sightstealer story without requiring a gravship overhaul.
