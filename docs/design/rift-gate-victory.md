# Rift Gate Victory

## Overview

The Sightstealer colony has a unique end-game victory condition: construct a **Rift Gate**, activate it, and survive the resulting attempt by the outside world to destroy it.

The Rift Gate represents the colony finally opening a stable passage between ordinary reality and the Rift.

It is not simply a transport building. It is the culmination of the Umbral research tree and the colony's entire progression.

---

# The Rift Gate

## Visual Design

The Rift Gate should be based directly on the visual concept of a large **Skipgate**, but redesigned to belong to the Sightstealer colony.

It should be:

- large
- imposing
- predominantly black
- visibly anomalous
- surrounded by subtle spatial distortion
- recognisably derived from the Skipgate silhouette
- free of bright conventional lighting

The final structure should look like the Sightstealers have taken an advanced piece of space-time technology and turned it into something that belongs to the Rift.

---

# Construction Requirements

The Rift Gate must be extremely expensive.

It should require large quantities of the colony's specialised resources, making construction a genuine end-game project rather than something the player can rush immediately after unlocking the research.

### Required materials

The exact numbers should be configurable, but the intended scale is:

- **Large quantity of Condensed Void**
- **Large quantity of Riftstone**
- **Large quantity of Umbral Shards**
- **Large quantity of Shadow Resin**
- **Large quantity of Twisted Fibre**
- **Large quantity of Void Sap-derived materials where technically appropriate**
- **Dark Archeotech Shards**

Dark Archeotech Shards are particularly important.

They should be recovered from **Anomaly creatures** and represent anomalous material that cannot simply be farmed from the Rift biome.

This forces the player to engage with Anomaly content before the victory condition can be completed.

---

# Dark Archeotech Shards

## Source

Dark Archeotech Shards should primarily drop from significant Anomaly creatures.

They should not normally appear as ordinary terrain resources or common Rift plants.

Potential sources include:

- major Anomaly entities
- dangerous anomalous creatures
- selected high-tier Anomaly encounters
- potentially major Anomaly event rewards

The exact drop rates should be balanced during testing.

## Purpose

Dark Archeotech Shards are one of the key components of the Rift Gate.

Their purpose is to connect the colony's two technological paths:

**Umbral technology + Anomaly technology = Rift Gate**

---

# Construction Process

The Rift Gate should be treated as a major project.

Recommended flow:

1. Complete the required Umbral research.
2. Acquire the required Rift resources.
3. Hunt/defeat sufficient Anomaly creatures to obtain Dark Archeotech Shards.
4. Construct the Rift Gate.
5. Begin the final defence period.
6. Survive for 15 days.
7. Win the game.

The player should receive clear notification when construction is completed and the defence period begins.

---

# The Final Defence

Once the Rift Gate is completed, the victory condition enters a **15-day defence phase**.

The colony must keep the Rift Gate operational and prevent it from being destroyed until the timer expires.

## Duration

**15 consecutive days.**

The timer should be clearly visible to the player.

The countdown should pause or fail if the gate is destroyed, according to the final implementation chosen by the developer. The preferred design is that destruction **fails the victory attempt** rather than silently continuing.

---

# Why The Defence Exists

The completed Rift Gate should dramatically increase the colony's Visibility.

The outside world can no longer ignore what the Sightstealers are doing.

The gate is effectively a beacon announcing:

> **The Rift is opening.**

This should trigger The Dark One's strongest escalation systems.

The final defence is therefore the ultimate expression of the Visibility loop.

---

# Final Defence Threats

The defence should use the existing storyteller system rather than requiring an entirely separate raid framework.

The Dark One should increase the severity and frequency of threats while the gate is active.

Potential attackers include:

- normal hostile factions
- Anomaly threats
- The Purifiers
- high-Visibility raids
- special anti-Rift attacks

The Purifiers should be particularly important during this phase because their doctrine is specifically suited to destroying a Sightstealer gate.

---

# Purifier Behaviour During The Defence

The Purifiers should prioritise:

1. Reaching the Rift Gate.
2. Destroying or damaging it.
3. Setting surrounding structures on fire.
4. Flooding the battlefield with light.
5. Killing Sightstealers defending the gate.

Their golden armour and fire-heavy equipment should make them visually distinct during the final battle.

They should be capable of making the final defence substantially more dangerous than ordinary raids.

---

# Gate Failure

The Rift Gate is the victory structure, so its destruction should matter enormously.

Preferred behaviour:

- Gate destroyed before completion of the 15-day timer: **victory attempt fails**.
- The player survives but the gate is destroyed: the player must rebuild it and begin a new 15-day defence.
- Resources are consumed when constructing the gate and must be reacquired if the gate is destroyed.
- A rebuilt gate starts a fresh 15-day defence.

The player should receive a clear message explaining that the Rift Gate has been lost and that the final defence must be restarted.

Do not permanently end the entire colony merely because the first gate is destroyed. The colony should be able to attempt the project again.

---

# Victory

After the Rift Gate has remained operational for **15 consecutive days**, the colony wins.

The victory should be treated as a custom RimWorld victory condition.

Suggested victory text:

> The gate stabilises.
>
> For fifteen days, the colony has held the boundary against everything that sought to close it. Now the Rift is no longer a distant place, a hostile biome, or something glimpsed through anomalous phenomena.
>
> The boundary has been breached.
>
> The Sightstealers step into the darkness beyond reality.
>
> The veil is gone.

The exact final text can be refined later.

---

# Victory Philosophy

This ending should represent the Sightstealer colony completing its ideological and technological journey.

The progression is:

**Survive in darkness**

-> **Understand the Rift**

-> **Build with Rift materials**

-> **Master psychic and anomalous forces**

-> **Acquire Dark Archeotech**

-> **Construct the Rift Gate**

-> **Become exposed to the entire world**

-> **Defend the gate**

-> **Cross the boundary**

The colony does not escape the planet by building a conventional ship.

It escapes the limitations of ordinary reality.

---

# Integration With The Dark One

The Rift Gate should be one of the strongest possible interactions with The Dark One's Visibility and Favour systems.

Construction can:

- sharply increase Visibility
- increase Favour
- unlock special storyteller events
- trigger unique dialogue/event text

During the 15-day defence, Visibility should effectively be treated as extremely high or fully exposed.

The Dark One should not allow the final phase to be quiet.

The player has spent the game deciding when to remain hidden and when to embrace exposure. The Rift Gate removes that choice:

> **Once the gate is opened, everyone knows.**

---

# Design Principle

The Rift Gate should feel like a true RimWorld end-game objective, not simply another expensive building.

The player should look at it and understand:

> **Everything has led to this.**

It combines:

- the Rift biome
- Rift materials
- Umbral research
- Anomaly creatures
- Dark Archeotech
- The Dark One
- Visibility
- Favour
- the Purifiers
- the Sightstealer ideology

into one final event.

The 15-day defence is the final test of whether the colony has genuinely become a force capable of opening the Rift.
