# Rift Flora

## Design Philosophy

The Rift should contain a recognisable ecosystem rather than a collection of resource nodes.

Some plants should provide useful materials, while others exist primarily to make the biome visually distinctive and alive.

Rift plants should generally thrive in low-light conditions and fit the existing Sightstealer theme of anomalous, organic darkness.

## Resource-Producing Plants

### Gloomroot

**Appearance:** Low-growing black roots spreading across the ground in tangled bundles. Some roots protrude from the soil like exposed tendons.

**Harvest:** Primarily Twisted Fibre, with a small amount of Shadow Resin.

**Purpose:** Basic renewable fibre crop for the Rift colony.

### Umbral Reed

**Appearance:** Tall, thin black stalks with narrow drooping leaves. Tips may have extremely subtle violet/blue highlights.

**Harvest:** Twisted Fibre, with a small chance of Umbral Shards.

**Purpose:** Renewable fibre and a visually distinctive tall crop.

### Dread Bloom

**Appearance:** Large dark flower with black/purple petals surrounding an unsettling central structure. The flower should feel vaguely organic without literally being a mouth or eye.

**Harvest:** Dread Petals.

**Purpose:** Psychic and ritual reagent.

### Shadowcap

**Appearance:** Large dark mushrooms with broad irregular caps. The underside can contain tiny points of faint anomalous colour.

**Harvest:** Void Sap or another suitable fungal product.

**Purpose:** Important food/biological resource for the Sightstealer colony and source of Void Sap.

### Bleakvine

**Appearance:** Long black vines that spread across ground, rocks, and structures. They should resemble dark veins.

**Harvest:** Shadow Resin.

**Special behaviour:** Mature Bleakvine may slowly spread across adjacent structures or terrain, creating an increasingly overgrown Rift aesthetic. This should be implemented only if the visual spread is technically and performance-wise practical.

### Hollowtree

**Appearance:** Large leafless twisted tree with a hollow or deeply distorted trunk. Branches should resemble fingers or tendrils. Subtle purple growths may emerge from the bark.

**Harvest:** Large quantities of Shadow Resin and Twisted Fibre; potentially Void Sap when mature.

**Purpose:** High-value renewable Rift plant.

### Rift Thorn

**Appearance:** Low sprawling plant covered in large hooked black thorns that resemble claws.

**Harvest:** Twisted Fibre and small amounts of Rift-related material.

**Special behaviour:** Can damage ordinary pawns and animals moving through it. Sightstealers should be unaffected.

**Purpose:** Natural defensive vegetation and colony perimeter protection.

## Decorative Rift Flora

These plants should not all be resource sources. Their main purpose is to make the Rift biome feel like a genuine ecosystem.

### Shadow Grass

Dense, almost-black grass forming dark patches across the ground.

### Pale Creeper

Thin grey-white vines growing over rocks and other surfaces. It provides contrast against the otherwise dark biome.

### Void Moss

Low carpet-like dark moss growing over soil and stone.

### Whisper Fungus

Small thin-stemmed mushrooms clustered in sheltered areas.

### Black Lichen

Dark patches growing over exposed stone and Riftstone-like surfaces.

## Biome Distribution

The Rift biome should use a mixture of these plants rather than placing every species everywhere.

Suggested distribution:

- Shadow Grass: very common.
- Void Moss: common.
- Gloomroot: common.
- Umbral Reed: common/uncommon.
- Shadowcap: common in suitable damp areas.
- Pale Creeper: uncommon.
- Black Lichen: common on rocks.
- Bleakvine: uncommon.
- Dread Bloom: uncommon/rare.
- Rift Thorn: uncommon, especially around rough terrain.
- Hollowtree: rare.

## Cultivation

At least the main resource-producing plants should be cultivable by the Sightstealer colony after appropriate research or from the start where balance permits.

The colony should therefore be able to establish a sustainable Rift ecosystem rather than relying entirely on wild plants.

## Light Relationship

Rift plants should generally tolerate or prefer low light.

This should not require every plant to have a unique custom light mechanic. Where possible, use normal plant growth systems with appropriate fertility, temperature, and biome settings.

The visual and mechanical identity should come primarily from their ability to thrive in the same darkness that benefits Sightstealers.
